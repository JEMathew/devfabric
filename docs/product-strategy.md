# Product Strategy

## Where to play

Start with teams that already own enterprise APIs and must share them across application and agent workflows. The invoice reminder provides observable failure cases. A settled invoice should be skipped, a revoked grant should block the next call and a sensitive reminder should wait for review. The other four business cases let teams rehearse the pattern. Invoicing has the connected operational workflow.

## How to win

**Self-Service Infrastructure** brings ownership, catalog discovery, a standard request and the administrator's decision into one record trail. **Agent-Ready Enterprise APIs** makes a tool discoverable through a reviewed contract, then checks authorization again at invocation. Sharing those records lets a reviewer reconstruct what happened without asking each team to retell the story.

Build the parts that express business judgment, including invoice eligibility, revision-bound decisions, task evaluation and explanations of denials. Reuse catalog entries, templates and the same command model across workspaces. For a pilot, integrate established identity, infrastructure, delivery and telemetry providers behind these interfaces. Operating those services is outside the current product. The first iteration favors reproducible failures and inspectable evidence over breadth of integrations.

Start adoption with the Template Gallery and isolated tours. Observe whether a team returns to the same path, whether a reviewer can explain a denial and whether an operator can recover a blocked run before expanding the pilot. [Pilot measures](metrics-and-outcomes.md)
