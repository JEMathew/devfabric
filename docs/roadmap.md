# Product Roadmap

These horizons describe proposed investments and the evidence needed to advance. Only the browser-local foundation is available in the preview.

| Horizon | Product decision | Evidence to advance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current · local foundation | Exercise discovery, provisioning, publication, evaluation and review for one scenario. | Reproduce a pass, failed gate, rejection, revoked grant and duplicate suppression. |
| Near term · controlled pilot | Observe one team from first service to first staged build. Introduce test identity, server-side policy and durable audit records. | Verify access revocation, attribution, recovery and whether a reviewer can explain a decision from its trace. |
| Medium term · multi-team | Add tenant isolation, service objectives and contract lifecycle management across teams. | Verify tenant boundaries and recovery drills. Measure repeat adoption against an eligible cohort. |
| Long term · connected platform | Connect verified enterprise context, service dependencies and agent orchestration across products. | Validate data ownership, provenance and freshness before relying on context for consequential decisions. |

The Template Gallery and isolated tours give people a low-cost way to learn the pattern. Returning to an operational workspace requires explicit actions. The product's proposed pilot targets include first staging within one working day and tracing a decision within two minutes. Neither is an observed customer result.
