# Developer Journey

Obtain an attributable sandbox for a team responsible for Invoice API. Start from **Open Workspace**, or enter the developer workspace from the invoice reminder case.

| Stage and trigger | Who acts and what they do | Output and value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A business case needs a service owner | The developer registers the team, accountable owner and product domain for Invoice API. | The catalog ties the service to a team. The next request has an accountable owner. |
| The team needs the correct interface | Inspect Invoice API and select its contract in the onboarding workflow. | The service and contract identify what the team intends to use. |
| Development needs a sandbox | Request sandbox access. The administrator opens the team's request in the access queue. | A numbered request makes the handoff and pending decision visible. |
| Access requires a decision | The administrator approves or rejects the request. After approval, start and complete simulated provisioning. | A ready environment records the handoff. A rejection or failed allocation remains in history so the team can correct and retry. |
| The team needs evidence | Inspect the request and environment, then follow progress in Journey & Friction Explorer. | Another persona can trace the outcome to the original request. No external infrastructure is allocated. |

For a template-based infrastructure request, **Infrastructure Self-Service** also exposes the proposed plan, policy and estimated cost before submission. **My Requests** shows allowed actions. **Environments** shows the resulting resource. A plan-level policy decision and an onboarding access approval apply to their own records.


The case reference survives the handoff. A practice approval never appears in the access queue. [Explore the service catalog](https://feature-devfabric-platform-e.enablement-hub.pages.dev/developer/service-catalog)
