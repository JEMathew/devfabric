# Product Vision

**From Business Intent to Trusted Software Delivery.** A developer and an agent working on the same customer problem need to find the same contract, understand who owns it and explain the outcome of using it. DevFabric's vision is to make that chain of decisions accessible to both.

Consider a billing team that wants to remind customers about overdue invoices. The developer needs a compliant sandbox and a dependable interface. The agent operator needs to know which tool can answer an eligibility question, under whose authority and with what evidence. A reviewer needs the invoice, contract revision, actual result and decision together.

Today, teams can exercise those handoffs in a browser-local workspace with synthetic records. The longer-term vision adds verified enterprise context, service dependencies and orchestration across teams. Progress depends on trusted identity, durable evidence and measured customer outcomes.
